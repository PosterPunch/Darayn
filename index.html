Arabic

لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ

Transliteration

Labbayk Allāhumma labbayk, labbayk lā sharīka laka labbayk, inna al-ḥamda wa an-ni‘mata laka wal-mulk, lā sharīka lak.

Translation

Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. Truly all praise, favour and sovereignty belong to You. You have no partner.

Begin once you have made your intention for Umrah, and say it often as you travel to the Haram.

Dua companion

Know what is established. Ask in your own words.

Open Darayn during Tawaf and find supplications that are authentically reported, each with where it comes from. We do not assign an invented dua to every circuit, so you never feel you are missing a required wording.

Authentically reported

From the Qur’an or reported from the Prophet ﷺ, with the source shown beside it.

General dua

Suggestions you can make in your own words. Always labelled, never presented as the required words for a circuit.

For every pilgrim

Umrah looks different for every family.

A wheelchair user, an elderly parent, a family with young children and someone travelling alone all need different things. Darayn plans for each of them.

Travelling with an elderly parent?

Plan a gentler pace, build in rest every day, and know where to find help and accessible routes before you need them.
